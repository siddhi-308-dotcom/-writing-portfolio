# The Role of IoT in Everyday Life

Have you ever wondered how a smartwatch tracks your daily activity or how a smart home device responds to your commands? These are examples of the Internet of Things, commonly known as IoT.

IoT connects physical devices to the internet so they can collect and exchange information. Sensors play an important role by collecting details such as temperature, humidity and air quality.

During my academic projects, I explored how sensor data can be used for monitoring and generating alerts. It helped me understand that even a small device can make a difference when it is used for the right purpose.

IoT is useful in areas such as agriculture, healthcare, smart homes and industries. At the same time, security and data privacy must be considered because connected devices handle information.

In my opinion, IoT has great potential to make everyday activities smarter, safer and more efficient.
